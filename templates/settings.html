Settings?Settings. Configure the Signal / Telegram / WhatsApp integrations, pick which groups & chats to monitor, and toggle general behaviour. Values written here live in the app_settings database table and override the matching .env defaults. Most apply immediately; a few (connector enable flags & the Telegram bot token) need a restart — the UI flags those.

Message capture?Controls what the poller writes to the database. "Save my own messages" stores messages your bot account sends (mirrored from a linked device) as if they were any other group member's — recommended ON so the operator's own contributions show in dashboards & analytics.

Signal link status?Talks to the signal-cli-rest-api daemon at SIGNAL_API_BASE. "Linked" means the daemon holds a registered Signal session for your number. If not linked, scan the QR code below from your phone (Signal → Settings → Linked devices → Link new device).

Checking…

Link a device (QR)?Generates a linking QR from signal-cli-rest-api. Open Signal on your phone → Settings → Linked devices → "Link new device" → scan. The daemon becomes a linked device of your account and starts receiving messages (and your own sent-message syncs). The QR is single-use and expires quickly — click "Get fresh QR" again if it times out.

Monitored groups?Lists every Signal group the linked account is in (from GET /v1/groups/<number>). Tick the groups you want archived & analysed; untick the rest. Saved here as the signal_target_group_ids setting, which overrides TARGET_GROUP_IDS in .env and is picked up by the poller within a few seconds — no restart needed. Leaving every box unticked means nothing is monitored.

Telegram connector status?Telegram is read via the Bot API — no QR code. Create a bot with @BotFather, copy its token, paste it below, and (in BotFather) disable privacy mode so the bot sees all group messages. The token is stored as telegram_bot_token; the tg-connector sidecar reads it at start, so changing the token or the enable flag needs a restart of the stack.

Bot configuration

Monitored chats?Lists chats the bot can see (from the connector's GET /v1/chats — available once the connector is running). Tick the ones to ingest. Saved as telegram_target_chat_ids; an empty selection in .env means "all chats the bot is in", but once you save here only the ticked chats are kept.

{# ─────────────────────────────── WHATSAPP ─────────────────────────────── #}

WhatsApp connector status?WhatsApp runs through the wa-connector sidecar (Baileys). It pairs as a linked device of a real WhatsApp account by scanning a QR (WhatsApp → Settings → Linked devices → Link a device). The session lives in the connector's volume, so you only pair once. Enabling/disabling the connector needs a restart.

Checking…

Pair a device (QR)?Fetches the current pairing QR from the connector. Scan it from WhatsApp → Linked devices. If the connector reports it's already linked, no QR is shown. The QR rotates — re-fetch if it doesn't take.

?Wipes the connector's stored WhatsApp session and forces a brand-new QR. Use this when you unlinked the device from your phone but the connector still won't show a QR. Existing message history in MySQL is not affected — only the linked-device pairing keys.
